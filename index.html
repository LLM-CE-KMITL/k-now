About K-NOW

A Nexus Built for Wisdom, Powered by AI

K-NOW (KMITL Nexus Of Wisdom) is a dedicated AI supercomputing laboratory established by the Department of Computer Engineering to accelerate discovery across the university. It brings high-performance GPU computing directly to students, faculty, and industry partners.

More than infrastructure, K-NOW is a hub where classrooms, research labs, and startups converge — turning raw compute into applied intelligence for Thailand and beyond.

What We Do

Three Missions, One Infrastructure

K-NOW exists to serve the full lifecycle of applied AI — from the classroom to the lab bench to real-world deployment.

01

Teaching & Learning

Hands-on GPU access for coursework, capstone projects, and workshops — giving students real infrastructure to build real AI systems, not just theory.

02

Research

Faculty and graduate researchers push the boundaries of machine learning, computer vision, NLP, and large-scale systems using dedicated compute resources.

03

Innovation

Startups, spin-offs, and industry collaborations prototype and validate AI products with production-grade compute, before scaling beyond the lab.
